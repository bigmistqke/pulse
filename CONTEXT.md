# pulse

The language of pulse lives in [`CANON.md`](CANON.md): each word is a term in its [Terms](CANON.md#terms) section, and what pulse does is stated by its specs. The conceptual model is the body of [`axiom-each-job-is-done-by-one-mechanism`](CANON.md#axiom-each-job-is-done-by-one-mechanism), and the history of what shipped is in [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md).

This file stays so that tools looking for a project glossary find the way there. When a word's meaning is settled, it becomes a term in `CANON.md`, not an entry here.
